## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- visitors to the public On2GO directory;
- customers who create an account, if that feature is enabled;
- Clover merchants who connect an establishment to On2GO;
- people who contact us for support.

The public directory feed is designed to contain only public restaurant information. It must not contain orders, customer information, totals or access tokens.

## 3. Information we may collect

### 3.1 Public merchant information

- business name, public address, hours and restaurant description;
- categories, items, prices and availability intended for public display;
- public ordering links;
- technical identifiers needed to match a listing to the correct Clover establishment.

### 3.2 Account and contact information

If an account is offered, we may collect a name, email address, telephone number and preferences the person chooses to save.

### 3.3 Order-related information

If On2GO participates in direct-order tracking, we may process the order number, establishment, items, status and contact details needed to communicate about the order. Payment is processed through the payment provider or flow configured by the merchant. On2GO does not ask for a full payment-card number in its directory.

### 3.4 Technical information

We may collect IP address, device or browser type, pages viewed, directory searches, error logs and session identifiers. If precise geolocation is offered in the future, it will be collected only with separate consent and may be refused without preventing a manual search.

### 3.5 Support communications

We keep emails, call notes and information voluntarily provided to handle a support request.

## 4. Purposes

We use personal information only to:

- operate and secure the directory;
- display the correct listing, public menu and ordering link;
- match a Clover merchant to its establishment;
- support order tracking when that feature is enabled;
- answer support requests;
- prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
- measure aggregate service use and correct errors;
- comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for targeted advertising without consent where consent is required.

## 5. Consent and choices

Where required, we seek clear, free, informed consent for specific purposes. You may withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawal may prevent use of a feature that depends on the information.

Essential cookies are used for security and operation. Any non-essential analytics or marketing cookies will be described in the final version and, where required, offered through a prior choice.

## 6. Disclosure to third parties

We disclose information only as necessary:

- to the restaurant selected by the customer, to handle the request or order;
- to Clover, when the merchant has connected an account and authorized the exchange;
- to hosting, security, analytics, communications and support providers bound by confidentiality obligations;
- to an authority or another party where required or permitted by law.

The final providers and their locations will be named or described by category in the approved version.

## 7. Transfers outside Québec

Some providers may process or store information outside Québec or Canada. Before such a transfer, we will complete the assessment required by Québec’s Law 25, consider contractual protections and inform individuals where required. Information remains subject to the laws of the jurisdiction where it is processed and may be accessible to authorities there.

## 8. Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described, security, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Exact retention periods by category **[ARE TO BE CONFIRMED]** before publication. At the end of the applicable period, information is securely destroyed, anonymized or archived according to its nature.

## 9. Security

We apply administrative, technical and physical safeguards proportionate to the sensitivity of the information: access controls, authentication, encryption in transit, logging, separation of public and private data, and tokens limited to authorized functions.

No system is completely secure. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec and affected individuals where required by law and will keep the required incident record.

## 10. Your rights

Subject to legal conditions, you may request:

- access to your personal information;
- correction of inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous information;
- cessation of dissemination or de-indexing in the cases provided by law;
- portability in a structured, commonly used technological format;
- withdrawal of consent;
- information about a decision based exclusively on automated processing, if such a decision is used.

Write to takatak.ca@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law, generally no later than 30 days after receiving a complete request. Complaints may also be made to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

## 11. Children

On2GO is not intended to knowingly collect children’s information outside a restaurant’s normal service flow. If you believe information was collected in error, contact us to request deletion.

## 12. Changes

We will publish material changes with a new update date and, where required, request new consent.
